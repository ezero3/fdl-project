# WM-811K Class-Imbalance Report

This report documents the complete task-05 experiment generated by [`examples/run_imbalance_experiment.py`](../../examples/run_imbalance_experiment.py) and analyzed in [`notebooks/05_class_imbalance_analysis.ipynb`](../../notebooks/05_class_imbalance_analysis.ipynb). Machine-readable results are stored under [`output/class_imbalance/`](../../output/class_imbalance/).

## Problem

The training split contains 121,063 labeled wafer maps, but 103,199 of them are `none`. The smallest class, `Near-full`, contains 104 maps. This produces an approximately **992:1** maximum imbalance and allows overall accuracy to conceal complete failure on minority patterns.

| Class | Training maps | Natural share |
|---|---:|---:|
| `Center` | 3,006 | 2.483% |
| `Donut` | 389 | 0.321% |
| `Edge-Loc` | 3,632 | 3.000% |
| `Edge-Ring` | 6,776 | 5.597% |
| `Loc` | 2,516 | 2.078% |
| `Near-full` | 104 | 0.086% |
| `Random` | 606 | 0.501% |
| `Scratch` | 835 | 0.690% |
| `none` | 103,199 | 85.244% |

Macro-F1 is therefore the primary selection metric: every class contributes equally, regardless of support.

## Leakage-safe protocol

The experiment uses:

- every one of the **121,063** fixed training rows;
- every one of the **34,591** validation rows in its natural distribution;
- the task-04 letterbox `64 x 64`, three-channel one-hot preprocessing;
- one 156,937-parameter spatial `BaselineCNN` for every intervention;
- AdamW, batch size 512, learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001, and at most four full epochs;
- best-checkpoint selection by validation macro-F1;
- unweighted validation cross-entropy and the complete task-03 metric set.

The large raw DataFrame is preprocessed once into an in-memory categorical `uint8` representation and converted to one-hot by batch. This is an exact optimization of the task-04 transform, not a dataset or modeling change.

The frozen test split was not loaded or inspected. The saved metadata explicitly records `test_set_used: false` and SHA-256 hashes of the train and validation row-index sequences.

## Interventions

Five mutually isolated strategies were screened with seed 86:

| Strategy | Training sampler | Training loss |
|---|---|---|
| `unweighted_ce` | Natural shuffle | Cross-entropy |
| `inverse_sqrt_ce` | Natural shuffle | Cross-entropy weighted by `1 / sqrt(n_c)` |
| `effective_number_ce` | Natural shuffle | Effective-number weighted cross-entropy, beta 0.9999 |
| `inverse_sqrt_sampler` | Weighted replacement sampling by `1 / sqrt(n_c)` | Unweighted cross-entropy |
| `focal_loss` | Natural shuffle | Focal loss, gamma 2 |

PyTorch cross-entropy accepts a per-class weight specifically for unbalanced training sets, while `WeightedRandomSampler` draws positions according to per-observation weights. The effective-number formulation follows Cui et al.; focal loss follows Lin et al. The strategies are not combined so their effects remain attributable.

## Screening result

| Rank | Strategy | Best epoch | Validation macro-F1 | Balanced accuracy | Accuracy |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | `inverse_sqrt_sampler` | 3 | 0.7888 | 0.8196 | 0.9526 |
| 2 | `inverse_sqrt_ce` | 4 | 0.7730 | 0.7896 | 0.9631 |
| 3 | `effective_number_ce` | 4 | 0.7681 | 0.8123 | 0.9570 |
| 4 | `unweighted_ce` | 3 | 0.7445 | 0.7400 | 0.9594 |
| 5 | `focal_loss` | 4 | 0.7194 | 0.6925 | 0.9583 |

The two best non-baseline strategies were advanced automatically. Focal loss and effective-number weighting remain implemented and documented, but did not consume additional confirmation runs.

## Three-seed confirmation

The baseline and finalists were evaluated over seeds 86, 87, and 88. Within a seed, model initialization and all non-intervention settings were identical.

| Strategy | Mean macro-F1 | Std. dev. | Mean balanced accuracy | Mean accuracy | Mean weighted-F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `inverse_sqrt_sampler` | **0.7955** | **0.0066** | **0.8106** | 0.9577 | **0.9588** |
| `inverse_sqrt_ce` | 0.7718 | 0.0127 | 0.7854 | **0.9600** | 0.9586 |
| `unweighted_ce` | 0.7286 | 0.0138 | 0.7087 | 0.9590 | 0.9544 |

Compared with the unweighted baseline, the selected sampler gains **0.0669 absolute macro-F1** and **0.1019 balanced accuracy**, while mean accuracy decreases by only **0.0013**. It also has the lowest macro-F1 variation across seeds.

## Selected sampling distribution

For class support `n_c`, each observation receives weight:

```text
w_c = 1 / sqrt(n_c)
```

Because a class has `n_c` observations, its total sampling probability is proportional to `sqrt(n_c)`. Sampling uses replacement and retains exactly 121,063 draws per epoch.

| Class | Natural share | Expected sampled share | Expected draws/epoch |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `Center` | 2.483% | 8.406% | 10,176 |
| `Donut` | 0.321% | 3.024% | 3,661 |
| `Edge-Loc` | 3.000% | 9.240% | 11,186 |
| `Edge-Ring` | 5.597% | 12.620% | 15,279 |
| `Loc` | 2.078% | 7.690% | 9,310 |
| `Near-full` | 0.086% | 1.564% | 1,893 |
| `Random` | 0.501% | 3.774% | 4,569 |
| `Scratch` | 0.690% | 4.430% | 5,363 |
| `none` | 85.244% | 49.252% | 59,626 |

This is deliberately less aggressive than equal-class sampling. It exposes the model to rare patterns more often while keeping approximately half of the epoch allocated to the diverse `none` class.

## Decision and usage

The shared training default is `DEFAULT_IMBALANCE_CONFIG`, corresponding to `inverse_sqrt_sampler`. It must be paired with ordinary unweighted cross-entropy:

```python
from torch import nn

from fdl_project import (
    DEFAULT_IMBALANCE_CONFIG,
    create_dataloader,
    create_imbalance_training_dataloader,
    create_split_dataset,
    load_wm811k_dataframe,
)

dataframe = load_wm811k_dataframe("data/MIR-WM811K/WM811K.pkl")
train_dataset = create_split_dataset(dataframe, "data/splits", "train")
validation_dataset = create_split_dataset(dataframe, "data/splits", "validation")

train_loader = create_imbalance_training_dataloader(
    train_dataset,
    batch_size=512,
    config=DEFAULT_IMBALANCE_CONFIG,
    seed=86,
)
validation_loader = create_dataloader(
    validation_dataset,
    batch_size=512,
    shuffle=False,
)
criterion = nn.CrossEntropyLoss()
```

The specialized loader rejects validation or test datasets. Save `imbalance_metadata(DEFAULT_IMBALANCE_CONFIG, counts)` in every future checkpoint so sampling assumptions remain traceable.

## Limitations

This experiment selects an imbalance policy for a controlled four-epoch baseline budget; it does not claim that the baseline is a final architecture or that the sampler is universally optimal. Later models must record the policy and may compare an alternative on validation if their behavior materially differs. Test must remain frozen until final model comparison.

Weighted sampling necessarily repeats rare observations, so the future augmentation task should examine physically valid transformations without changing validation/test. The remaining `Scratch` weakness also motivates spatial feature engineering in task 07.
